Good questions. Clear answers.

A little more peace of mind.

Do I need to pay anything?

No. Workers never pay Kaam to find work. If a venue asks for a deposit, uniform fee, or money for an interview, report it in the app.

Which jobs can I find?

Fast food cook, line cook, tandoorchi, waiter, dishwasher, barista, and helper roles at hospitality venues in Islamabad.

Who can see my number and voice introduction?

Employers can hear your voice introduction as part of the hiring process. Your phone number stays private until you agree to a call.

Where do I sign up?

Worker signup and job invitations are in the Kaam Android app. This website explains the process; it does not have a worker account form. The pilot app is shared directly by the Kaam team.

What if the salary or shift changes at the interview?

Report “Tankhwah jhoot thi” or “Shift badal di” in the app. You can also report a request for money or a seat that has already been filled.
