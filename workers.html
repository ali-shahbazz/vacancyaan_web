01

A few taps. A real introduction.

Open the Kaam Android app and choose Mujhe kaam chahiye. Verify your phone, confirm you’re 18 or older, and choose your role, area, salary, and shift. Add your name and a short voice introduction.

02

The job details, clear from the start.

Browse jobs with the role, salary and travel shown first. Apply, or accept an invitation from a venue that wants to talk to you.

03

Tap Haan, mujhe call karein if you’d like a call. Your number is shared only after you agree. Save the venue’s number so you know who’s calling.

Your questions, answered

Do I need to pay anything?

No. Workers never pay Kaam to find work. If a venue asks for a deposit, uniform fee, or money for an interview, report it in the app.

Which jobs can I find?

Cooks, tandoorchis, waiters, dishwashers, baristas and helpers at restaurants, cafés and hotels, plus cashiers, shelf packers, loaders, delivery riders and pharmacy assistants at shops, across Pakistan.

Who can see my number and voice introduction?

Employers can hear your voice introduction as part of the hiring process. Your phone number stays private until you agree to a call.

Where do I sign up?

Worker signup and job invitations are in the Kaam Android app. This website explains the process; it does not have a worker account form. The pilot app is shared directly by the Kaam team.

What if the salary or shift changes at the interview?

Report “Tankhwah jhoot thi” or “Shift badal di” in the app. You can also report a request for money or a seat that has already been filled.
