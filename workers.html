Do I have to pay anything?

No. Workers never pay Work Pakistan. No venue may ask for a deposit, a uniform fee or money for an interview.

Do I need a CV?

No. Your answers and your voice introduction are what employers see.

Who can hear my voice introduction?

Employers whose jobs fit you. Before a venue pays, it can hear up to three matching workers, with initials only; you can turn that off in Settings.

When does an employer get my number?

Only after you say yes: when you apply and they accept, or when you accept their invite.

Which jobs are there?

Cooks, tandoorchis, waiters, dishwashers, baristas and helpers at restaurants, cafés and hotels, and cashiers, shelf packers, loaders, delivery riders and pharmacy assistants at shops, across Pakistan.

Why does the app ask if I am still looking?

If you have not used it for three days, it asks once. If you do not answer within a day, venues stop seeing you until you come back, so they only call people who are looking.
