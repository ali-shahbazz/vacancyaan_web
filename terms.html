Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and use your own phone number. Employers must have the right to hire for the venue they list.

Community rules

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. You must not:

  • Charge, or ask for, any money, deposit or cut from a worker for a job, training, uniform or placement.
  • Post a fake job, misstate the salary, shift or conditions, or pretend to be a business or person you are not.
  • Offer work that is unlawful, exploitative, forced, or that involves trafficking or anyone under 18.
  • Harass, threaten, abuse or discriminate against anyone, or share sexual, hateful or violent content, in any name, photo, voice recording or call.
  • Use a worker's details for anything other than hiring for the job they were shared for, or pass them to anyone else.
  • Try to get around the app's privacy, limits or security.

Reporting and enforcement

You can report any job or worker in the app, and block anyone. The team reviews reports and acts on those that break these rules. We can remove content, hide a venue, close a job, and suspend an account without notice, and a suspended number cannot sign up again. If you think we made a mistake, email contact@cresra.com.

What Vacancyaan does and does not do

We connect workers and venues. We are not the employer and we are not party to any job. "Payment verified" means a venue's payment to us was confirmed. It does not mean we inspected the venue. We do not guarantee that a job, person or salary is genuine, or that anyone will be hired. Take care: meet in a public place and never pay for a job.

Workers

Workers never pay Vacancyaan. Give true details, and keep your availability up to date.

Employers and payment

Prices and what each plan includes are shown before you pay. A job goes live after the payment is confirmed. In the app you pay through Google Play or the App Store, and their refund rules apply to those purchases. A job, pack or monthly plan runs for the period shown, and the contacts it includes end with it. Workers' contact details are shared only with their consent. A monthly plan bought in the app renews until you cancel it in your store account. Refunds through a store end what the purchase paid for. Other replacements and disputes follow the rules shown in the app.
