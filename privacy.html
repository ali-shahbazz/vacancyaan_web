Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026. This policy covers the Work Pakistan app and website, a private service run by Cresra. It is not a government service.

What we collect, and why

  • Phone number: to sign you in with a code, and to share with the other side once you agree to talk.
  • Workers: name, the work you do, secondary roles, what you say you have (such as a motorbike), area, experience, expected salary, shift, whether you need a place to stay, gender if you give it, your answer that you are 18 or older, a voice introduction, and a photo if you add one. These are used to match you with jobs and to show employers who you are.
  • CNIC number: optional. If you add it, it is stored encrypted. Only you can read it in the app, and it is not shared with employers. The last four digits are stored separately. It is not checked with NADRA.
  • Guarantor: if you add one, their name, phone and relation stay private to you. Employers only see that you can provide a guarantor. Please ask your guarantor first.
  • Employers: venue names, area, map link, team members' phone numbers, jobs you post, notes you write about applicants, and the payment references, store order numbers and receipts you send.
  • Activity: applications, invites, interviews, screening answers, saved jobs and searches, reports, blocks, and whether someone was hired.
  • Device and technical data: a Firebase installation ID, device platform and language so we can send notifications, whether a notification was opened, your sign-in sessions, and your IP address in server logs and rate limits.

We do not use your location from your phone's GPS. You choose your area from a list. We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not sell your data.

Who can see what

  • Your phone number goes to a venue only after you say yes: by applying and being accepted, or by accepting an invite. We never sell lists of phone numbers. The venue's phone number is shown to you at the same point.
  • Employers who have paid for a live job see the initials and work details of workers who fit that job and can listen to their voice introduction. Your name and photo are shown only when you apply to that venue or accept its invite.
  • Employers who have not paid can hear a small number of voice introductions, shown with initials only and no photo or phone number. You can turn this off in Settings. Do not say your name or number in your voice introduction.
  • Workers can see a job's details and the venue's name, area and map link.
  • The Work Pakistan team can see what is needed to review payments, reports and disputes, including listening to a voice introduction. These actions are recorded.

Companies that handle data for us

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications. It receives your installation ID and the notification's text.
  • An SMS provider delivers sign-in codes. It receives your phone number and the code.
  • Our hosting provider stores the service. Servers may be outside Pakistan.
  • Google Maps services and a map lookup service may receive a map link or place name an employer enters, to fill in a location.

When you tap to call, open WhatsApp or open a map, that app handles what you do next under its own policy. In the app, employers pay through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment and give us an order reference, never card details. On the website, employers pay by bank or Raast transfer through their own bank.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile, voice and photo: until you change them or delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes: one day. Notifications: 90 days.
  • Payment records and receipts: as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Reports, dispute records and the record of team actions: as long as needed for safety and to answer complaints.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves backups as they expire.

On Android, a sign-in key may be saved with your Google backup so that you stay signed in on a new phone. It holds no phone number or profile, and it is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Your choices

  • Change your profile, voice introduction and photo at any time, or remove the photo.
  • Turn off the voice preview, and choose which notifications you get, in Settings.
  • Block a venue or worker. You stop seeing each other.
  • See your signed-in devices and sign out of all of them.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at contact@cresra.com.

Deleting your account

Delete your account in Settings, or see how to delete your account if you cannot sign in. This signs you out everywhere and removes your phone number, name, CNIC, guarantor, voice, photo, saved items, notes about you, notifications and device records. We keep job, application, payment, report and audit records without your phone number, for the reasons above. If an account was suspended or under review, we keep a coded version of the number, which cannot be read back, so it cannot sign up again.

Children

Work Pakistan is for people aged 18 and over. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, report it in the app or email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, CNIC numbers are encrypted when stored, and team access is recorded. No service can promise perfect security.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is important.

Location information

City names come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Area and address information comes from © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Maps suggestions can be edited and do not verify a venue.
