Privacy, in plain words.

Work Pakistan is built so that a worker decides who can call them. This page says what the app keeps and who can see it.

What we keep

  • Your phone number, used to sign in with a code.
  • For workers: your work, area, experience, the salary and shift you want, your name, your CNIC number, a voice introduction and, if you add one, a photo.
  • For employers: the venue’s name, area and map link, the jobs you post, and the payment references and receipts you send.
  • What happens on a job: applications, invites, interviews, whether someone was hired, and reports.

Who sees what

  • A worker’s phone number is shared with a venue only after the worker says yes: by applying and being accepted, or by accepting the venue’s invite. There are no lists of numbers to download.
  • Employers see a worker’s hiring details and can listen to the voice introduction. Before paying, a venue can hear up to three matching workers with initials only; workers can turn this off in Settings.
  • A worker’s CNIC number is stored encrypted and only the worker can see it. It is not shared with employers and is not a NADRA check.
  • Work Pakistan’s operations team reviews payments, reports and disputes, and every decision they take is recorded.

Your choices

  • Change your profile, voice introduction and photo at any time, or remove the photo.
  • See your signed-in devices and sign out of all of them.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This signs you out everywhere and removes your profile, CNIC and media; payment and credit records the business must keep are retained without your phone number.

Location information

City names come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Area and address information comes from © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Maps suggestions can be edited and do not verify a venue.
