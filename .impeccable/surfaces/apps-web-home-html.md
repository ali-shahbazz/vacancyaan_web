# Public website (home, employers, workers)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: restaurant, café, hotel and shop owners hiring one person (primary payer), and workers looking for a job (free, often Urdu readers on budget phones). Action: employers "Start hiring" → /app/?start=employer; workers "Find work" → /app/?start=worker; both open real sign-up in the web app. Proof: real product mechanics only (consent before contact, workers never pay, one seat per job, refunds on decline/expiry, voice introductions, Metro ride matching, three languages, plans PKR 2,000 / 7,000 / 20,000). No customers, numbers, testimonials or press exist. Contact: contact@cresra.com; "A product by Cresra".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every job is a painted truck panel that travels Pakistan: role, pay and shift framed in precise ornament, signed by the venue. Refuses the category default of headline + phone mockup + three feature cards on white.

OWN-WORLD: Deep bottle-green panels (#0E3B2A, deeper #082519) as the ground, lime #D7F597 as the brand highlight, marigold #F2B21B ornament line, vermilion #D9412B chevron tape, mirror-silver facets. Ornament borders drawn as crisp vector on one 8-unit lattice; arched medallions; painted plaques as buttons; tabular numerals for every salary, shift and distance. Urdu lettering is a first-class voice.

STORY: The visitor sees one real job shape and the rule (one yes before the call), understands it is free for workers and paid per hire for venues, follows the four-panel procession (post, match, yes, call), trusts the rules page-section, then takes the plaque for their side.
