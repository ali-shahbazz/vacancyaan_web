The rules, painted on the back.

ہر کام کی عزت

  • Workers never pay.

    No sign-up fee, no job fee, no deposit. If a venue asks for money, the worker reports it in the app and the venue is hidden while Work Pakistan reviews it.

  • No number without a yes.

    A phone number is shared only after the worker applies and the venue accepts, or the worker accepts the venue’s invite. There are no lists to download.

  • One job, one seat, one salary.

    Each post is for one person, with the pay and shift in writing. Workers can report a salary that was false or a shift that changed.

  • Venues pay for a hire, not for data.

    A payment covers a set number of calls to workers who agreed. Declined and expired invites give the credit back.

  • A CNIC stays with its owner.

    Workers add their CNIC number privately. It is stored encrypted and never shown to employers. It is not a NADRA check.

  • Three languages, read aloud.

    English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, and every sign-up question can be heard in Urdu.

Questions people ask first.

Something else? Write to us at contact@cresra.com

Is it really free for workers?

Yes. Workers never pay Work Pakistan, and no venue may ask for a deposit, a uniform fee or money for an interview. If one does, report it in the app.

How do I start?

Tap Start hiring or Find work. The app opens in your browser: enter your phone number, type the code we send, and answer one question at a time. The same account works in the Android app.

When does an employer get a worker’s number?

Only after the worker says yes: by applying and being accepted, or by accepting the venue’s invite. Before that, the venue sees experience and area and can hear the voice introduction.

How do employers pay?

By Raast or bank transfer on a checkout page. You submit the transaction reference, and the job goes live when Work Pakistan matches it to the bank record, usually the same working day. If it cannot be matched, you see why and can send a corrected reference.

What if a worker says no or does not answer?

The invite’s credit goes back to the venue. Credits are only used when a worker agrees to talk.

Which cities?

Anywhere in Pakistan. Workers and venues choose their city and area; jobs match within the same city, and Rawalpindi and Islamabad also match along the Metrobus.

Does Work Pakistan check venues?

A venue’s job goes live only after its payment is matched, and a worker’s report of a fee request hides the venue while it is reviewed. Work Pakistan does not inspect venues in person and does not employ workers.

Who can see a worker’s CNIC?

Only the worker. It is stored encrypted, never shown to employers, and is not a NADRA check.
