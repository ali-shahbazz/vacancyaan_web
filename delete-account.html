Delete your Work Pakistan account

You can delete your Work Pakistan account, and the data listed below, at any time. This works for worker and employer accounts.

اکاؤنٹ ڈیلیٹ کرنے کے لیے ایپ کھولیں: پروفائل، پھر سیٹنگز، پھر ڈیلیٹ اکاؤنٹ۔ اگر آپ لاگ ان نہیں کر سکتے تو اپنا فون نمبر لکھ کر contact@cresra.com پر ای میل کریں۔

If you can sign in

  1. Open the Work Pakistan app, or sign in on the web.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every device.

If you cannot sign in

Email contact@cresra.com with the subject "Delete my Work Pakistan account" and the phone number on the account. We will confirm that the number is yours before deleting anything, and aim to finish within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number and sign-in records
  • Your name, CNIC, guarantor, gender, voice introduction and photo
  • Venue names, map link and photo, for venues that close with the account
  • Saved jobs and searches, screening answers, blocks, notes employers wrote about you, notifications and device records

Open jobs close, interviews are cancelled, and a head office account closes its branches. Unused credits end. If you have paid and a transfer is still being checked, email us first.

What is kept, and why

  • Job, application, wallet and payment records, including receipts, without your phone number on your account: as long as accounting and tax law requires. A receipt image may itself show details you sent.
  • Reports, disputes and the record of team actions: as long as needed for safety and to answer complaints.
  • For accounts suspended or under review, a coded version of the number, which cannot be read back, so it cannot sign up again.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves backups as they expire.

See the Privacy Policy for more.
