Know what happens next.

How do I post a job?

Use the Work Pakistan Android app, choose the employer role, and complete your venue and job details. The pilot app is shared directly by the Work Pakistan team. This website does not create employer accounts.

How do I pay?

Your job opens a secure, time-limited invoice in the browser. Transfer the amount shown using Raast or the bank details, then submit your transaction reference. The invoice is for one person’s hiring service.

Is my job live as soon as I submit a reference?

No. The job stays pending until Work Pakistan operations match your transfer to the bank record, usually the same working day. The app notifies you when it goes live. If the transfer cannot be matched, you see the reason and can submit a corrected transaction ID.

Can I see phone numbers before a worker agrees?

No. Payment enables the hiring process; contact details are shared only after the worker’s consent. You cannot download a list of phone numbers.

What happens if someone declines my invitation?

The invitation credit is returned when a worker declines or the invitation expires. Credit rules and replacement eligibility are enforced by the server, so retrying an action does not charge you twice.
