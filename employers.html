From a post to a hire, one step at a time.

Everything happens in the Work Pakistan app, on your phone or in the browser. Payment opens on a checkout page.

  1. 1

    Set up your venue

    Name, area and a Google Maps link, so workers know where they would travel. Chains can add branches and managers later.

  2. 2

    Describe the seat

    Role, salary range, shift, meals and a place to stay. One post is one person.

  3. 3

    Ask up to three questions

    Yes-or-no questions, such as weekend work. A worker who answers no is shown other jobs instead of applying.

  4. 4

    Hear matching workers

    Before paying, listen to the voice introductions of up to three workers who fit. Initials only, and workers can turn this off.

  5. 5

    Pay by Raast or bank transfer

    Transfer the amount on the checkout page and send the transaction reference. The job goes live once Work Pakistan matches it, usually the same working day.

  6. 6

    Review and invite

    Applicants arrive with experience, area and voice. Accept to share numbers, or invite matching workers yourself.

  7. 7

    Talk, trial, hire

    Call or WhatsApp, schedule an interview or trial in the app, and record what happened.

  8. 8

    Get the credit back when it doesn’t work out

    If a worker declines your invite or doesn’t answer in time, the credit returns. You can also report a worker who could not be reached.

Several branches? Run them from one account.

Turn your venue into a head office, add branches, and invite a manager for each by phone number.

  • Managers see their branch.

    A branch manager hires only for their own branch. Head office sees every branch and its hiring.

  • One monthly pool.

    On the monthly plan, every branch draws from the same contacts, with an optional limit for each branch.

  • Stop when you want.

    Stopping the plan keeps every month you paid for. Switch back to paying per job when it ends, or keep it before then.

  • Every payment in one place.

    Plan and billing in the app lists each payment, what it paid for and whether it was matched.

Can I see phone numbers before I pay?

No. Before paying you can hear up to three matching workers’ voice introductions, with initials only. Numbers are shared only after a worker says yes to your job.

What happens after I pay?

Your job stays pending until Work Pakistan matches the transfer to the bank record, usually the same working day. The app tells you when it goes live. If the transfer cannot be matched, you see why and can send a corrected reference.

Can I change a job before paying?

Yes. An unpaid job can be edited until you pay. Once live, you can close it or post it again.

What if nobody suitable applies?

Invite matching workers directly from the job. Each invite holds one credit until the worker answers; a decline or no answer returns it.

Do I need a smartphone?

Any phone with a browser works, and there is an Android app. Sign in with your phone number and a code.

Does Work Pakistan employ the workers?

No. Work Pakistan helps you find and reach workers who agreed to talk. You agree the job, trial and pay directly with the person you hire.
